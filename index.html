Research

Current directions

My broader goal is to make generative models more practical: fewer sampling steps, steadier behaviour under imperfect data, and representations that hold up across modalities.

01

Diffusion language models

Few-step generation and tighter training–inference alignment for efficient sequence-to-sequence models.

  • few-step sampling
  • seq2seq
02

Multimodal learning

Shared representations that connect visual, textual, and temporal signals while preserving their complementary structure.

  • CLIP
  • mixture-of-experts
03

Time series analysis

Scenario-level guidance and terminal-step behaviour in diffusion forecasters, where denoising can work against the signal.

  • forecasting
  • guidance
