I am an AI Resident at FPT AI Center, working under the supervision of Prof. Tung Kieu and Prof. Tung Hoang-Thanh. I received my Bachelor's degree from HUST (Hanoi University of Science and Technology). My research lies at the edge of generative models, expanding from computer vision, language model to data mining, with a focus for efficient and timely problem.

I'm always open to collaborations, discussions, and new opportunities. Feel free to reach out if you're interested in my research or would like to discuss potential projects.

Research: My research centers on efficiency and model analysis for generative models, typically Diffusion Models, with particular emphasis on fast predictive model. I am also interested in frameworks related to the theoretical of time series analysis in data mining.

1. Reliable Inference. I develop guidance method for conditional generative models under different settings, including learning with noisy labels, sequence-to-sequence generation, and anomaly prediction, aiming to bridge the gap with real-world adaptation.

2. Foundation Model. Currently, I am also interested in building compacted foundation model for time series forecasting, that can generalize well on different data domain.
